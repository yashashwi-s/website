## What the referral data actually tells us

The supplied snapshot contains 107 referral events: 52 ChatGPT, 28 Reddit, 19 Google, 6 GitHub, and 2 Bing. ChatGPT accounts for about 49% of those events. The reporting period and conversion counts were not supplied. Unique visitors must not be summed across sources because the same person may appear in several groups.

This is evidence of visits attributed to ChatGPT, not proof of a crawler run, a ranking improvement, or installs. Separate website download clicks from GitHub asset download totals; neither measures successful installation. Keep website measurements separate from app telemetry. Older 2.4.9 downloads are telemetry-disabled; 2.5.0 enables limited weekly statistics. Do not link web visitors to installation UUIDs.

## Answer-engine visibility

Maintain accessible HTML, canonical URLs, working images, factual FAQs, and crawlable robots/sitemap routes. The local crawl check verifies those technical conditions; it cannot force another service to crawl or cite the app.

[Google’s AI search guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features) does not require a special AI schema or AI text file. [Anthropic distinguishes its search, user-retrieval, and training crawlers](https://privacy.claude.com/en/articles/8896518-does-anthropic-crawl-data-from-the-web-and-how-can-site-owners-block-the-crawler). Allowing them is not a guarantee of inclusion. Do not equate training access with discovery traffic.

Useful third-party coverage and accurate demonstrations are a stronger next investment than repetitive FAQ generation. Keep existing answers current; add a new answer when an actual user question exposes a gap.
